# Candy Design System
A vibrant, high-fidelity design system for premium portfolios.

## North Star: "Electric Playfulness"
The aesthetic is clean but alive with vibrant gradients, glassmorphism, and neon accents. It’s "Candy" because it’s visually sweet, high-contrast, and satisfying to interact with.

## Colors
- **Primary Background:** Deep Charcoal (#050505)
- **Primary Accent:** Neon Purple (#bc13fe)
- **Secondary Accent:** Electric Cyan (#00f2ff)
- **Tertiary Accent:** Candy Pink (#ff4d94)
- **Surface:** Translucent Glass (#ffffff0a) with Backdrop Blur (20px)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans — Bold and expressive.
- **Body:** Inter — Clean and readable.

## Shapes
- **Corner Radius:** 16px (rounded-2xl) for cards and buttons.
- **Borders:** 1px subtle glow or semi-transparent white.

## Elevation
- No hard shadows. Use **Glows** (Box-shadow with color) for active elements.
